length, bill depth, flipper length, body mass, sex, and year. The
species include Adelie, Gentoo, and Chinstrap. The islands include
Torgersen, Biscoe, and Dream. The dataset has 344 observations and 8
variables. The mean flipper length is 200.9152047mm.
